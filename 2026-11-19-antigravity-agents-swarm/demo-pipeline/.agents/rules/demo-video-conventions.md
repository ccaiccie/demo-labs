# Demo-Video Conventions

Follow these conventions for all demo-video work in this repository:

- **Narration voice**: Use the Kokoro `am_michael` voice.
- **Spoken-text pronunciation**: Spell `IP` as `eye pee` and present-tense `read` as `reed` in spoken text.
- **On-screen privacy & hygiene**: Never show API keys, secrets, emails, or desktop notifications on screen.
- **Audio loudness consistency**: Keep per-minute speech RMS within `1.5 dB`.
- **Video titles**: Keep video titles at `100` characters or fewer.
